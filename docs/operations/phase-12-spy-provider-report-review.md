## Findings

The report is not approved for expansion.

Primary blocker:

- The report's market and technical sections state `SPY` close `746.74` for `2026-06-18`.
- The Phase 12 direct Yahoo chart validation returned `SPY` close `549.33` for the same date.
- This is a severe data-grounding and consistency failure.

Secondary issues:

- Evidence labels are still generic.
- Options relevance remains placeholder-level.
- Fundamental and macro gaps are disclosed, but the report still depends heavily on unsupported narrative synthesis.
- The mapped trade plan is only `Overweight` wrapped in research-only language, which is too thin for reviewed research output.
- A non-blocking yfinance `fc.yahoo.com` warning still appears in outcome-resolution logging, even though the final persisted run completed.

Strengths:

- Chinese readability is acceptable.
- Research-only safety language is present.
- Missing fundamental and macro data are at least disclosed instead of fabricated.
